Data sources

The global wage comparison uses the ILO’s Global Wage Report and adjusts your pay for prices in your country or region of residence. Country rankings annualize ILOSTAT’s 2024 monthly earnings by multiplying by 12. Currency conversions use the annual average exchange rate for the comparison year.

Salary comparison questions

Where does USD 80,000 a year in the United States fall worldwide?
In the app’s example, USD 80,000 for a US resident falls roughly 5th to 10th from the top when the published wage benchmark is represented by 100 people. The estimate accounts for local prices.
Can I browse average salaries without entering my pay?
Yes. The rankings cover 76 countries using 2024 data. You can sort from highest to lowest or lowest to highest, or search by country name.
Is World Salary Map free?
Yes. The Android app is free and includes banner ads. Comparing and recording salaries does not require an account.
