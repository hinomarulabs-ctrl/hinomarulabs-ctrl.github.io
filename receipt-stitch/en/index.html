Frequently asked questions

Is Receipt Stitch free?

Yes. All features are free. The app displays ads and requires no account.

Can I import digital receipts?

Yes. Import digital receipts saved as PDFs or images. You can combine original PDF pages with photos of paper receipts in the same export.

Do I need an Excel spreadsheet?

Choose “Use receipts only” to create a PDF from the receipts you select, in the order you choose.

Can I correct a scan?

Yes. You can edit the date, amount, merchant, and other details. Scan accuracy depends on how clear the receipt is and how it’s laid out, so check the fields against the original before submitting.
