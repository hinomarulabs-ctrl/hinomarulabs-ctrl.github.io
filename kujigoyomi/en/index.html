Frequently asked questions

About Kujigoyomi

What is Kujigoyomi?

Kujigoyomi is an app that helps you explore Japanese lucky and caution days, plan lottery purchases, track spending, and review your records.

What is a lucky day in Japan?

A lucky day is a date traditionally considered auspicious for purchases, weddings, celebrations, or new beginnings. Interpretations can vary.

What do Ichiryū Manbaibi and Tensha-nichi mean?

They are names used for traditional Japanese calendar markers. Ichiryū Manbaibi is associated with growth and new beginnings, while Tensha-nichi is a rare day traditionally regarded as favorable for a fresh start.

Will a lucky day improve my odds of winning?

No. Lucky days are cultural reference information and do not guarantee or improve lottery odds.

Can I get reminders for planned purchases?

Yes. You can receive reminders for your planned purchase at a time you choose.

Can I track how much I spend?

Yes. You can record purchases, review your history, and set a monthly budget.
