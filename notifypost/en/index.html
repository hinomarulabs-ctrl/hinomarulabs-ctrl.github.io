FAQ

Can I see dismissed notifications?

Yes, if NotifyPost captured and saved them after saving was enabled. You can revisit those notifications even after dismissing them from Android’s notification panel. Notifications dismissed before saving started cannot be recovered. Android may prevent the app from capturing the content of some protected notifications.

Where is notification content stored?

Notification content, notes, and labels are stored on your device and are not sent to or stored on our servers. If you export your history, a file is created in the location you choose. An optional viewing lock protects access using biometrics or your device’s screen lock. This lock does not encrypt the stored data.

How do reminders in Later work?

Choose a date and time, then enable a reminder to be notified when it is time to revisit the item. Notification permission is required. Battery-saving settings and other device restrictions may delay the reminder.

Does this change the read status in the original app?

No. Reading or organizing a notification in NotifyPost does not change its read status in the original app.

Is NotifyPost free?

All features are free to use. NotifyPost requires Android 12 or later.
