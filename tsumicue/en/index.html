What should we build today?

Tsumicue is a 3D guide to building houses, trains, and castles with the blocks you already have. See which piece comes next and where it goes.

Free on Android

Pick it. Gather the blocks. Build it together.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a special set of blocks?
Use the blocks you have if their shapes and sizes match the model. The colors can be different. If you’re short a piece, tap “Missing a block?” in the app to choose another model.
Can we build without an internet connection?
Yes. Once the app is installed, you can use the models, 3D instructions, and voice guidance offline.
Can I turn off voice guidance?
Yes. You can switch voice guidance on or off in the app settings.
