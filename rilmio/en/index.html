Frequently asked questions

About Rilmio

Is Rilmio free?+

Yes. Rilmio is free to use.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. You can start using Rilmio without creating an account.

What is a routine timer?+

It is a timer for setting up several steps and planned durations, then moving through them one at a time.

Can I change a step’s time while a routine is running?+

Yes. You can change unstarted steps for the current run only. Your saved routine is not affected.

Can I change the order of steps?+

Yes. You can reorder steps that have not started yet for the current run only.

Does it move to the next step automatically when time is up?+

No. Overtime is shown after the planned time, and you stay on the current step until you complete it.

Can I resume after closing the app?+

Yes. You can restore a session that was in progress and continue from where you left off.

Can I use it without allowing notifications?+

Yes. You can use the timer without allowing notifications.

Where is my data stored?+

Routines and history are stored on your device. Data may not carry over when you change devices or reinstall the app.
