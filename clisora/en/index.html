KNOW YOUR APP

The important details,
before you begin.

Your business records stay on your device. Here is how Clisora handles your data, backups, and amounts.

Data storage
Customer and deal data is stored on your device. There is no automatic cloud sync.
Backups
Save and restore a password-protected encrypted backup manually. Keep a copy outside your device in case it is lost or damaged.
Platforms & languages
Available for iPhone. Android coming soon. The app supports English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
Amounts
Amounts are recorded as numbers without currency symbols, so you can use any currency. Clisora does not convert currencies.
Personal use
Team editing and automatic syncing between devices are not supported.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions

Who is Clisora for?

Clisora is for solo business owners and freelancers who handle their own sales and customer relationships, including makers, designers, consultants, and independent service providers.

Is Clisora really free?

Yes. You can use Clisora without a monthly fee or in-app purchases. Banner ads are shown in the app.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start managing customers and deals without registering an email address or password.

Where is my customer data stored?

Customer, deal, activity, and task data is stored on your device. Clisora does not automatically sync this data to the cloud. See the privacy policy for information about data related to advertising.

Can I back up my data when I change devices?

Yes. You can manually save and restore a password-protected encrypted backup. Keep the backup outside your device. If you forget the password, it cannot be reset and the backup cannot be decrypted.

Can I share data with my team or sync across devices?

Clisora currently does not support team editing or automatic syncing between devices. It is designed for managing customer information and deals on one person's device.

Will I receive task reminders?

Enable reminders in the app and allow notifications on your device to use them. Notification timing can be affected by device and operating system settings.

Can I use my own currency?

Yes. Amounts are recorded as numbers only, so you can use yen, dollars, euros, or any other currency. Clisora does not convert currencies, so keep amounts in the same currency when you add them up.
